# AI Assistance and Author Responsibility

Yes, I used AI to help write a field guide about AI. That probably shouldn't be the plot twist.

This guide is grounded in more than 25 years of my experience in IT and technology leadership. I bring the perspective, personal experiences, opinions, and decisions about what belongs in the guide. AI helps me organize those ideas, develop drafts, refine explanations, and prepare the Markdown files.

The writing process is collaborative and iterative. I contribute experiences, question the wording, challenge assumptions, and review and approve the material before publication. AI assistance extends to drafting, not just spelling and grammar.

The guide also draws on established practices, published research, and vendor documentation. Some examples illustrate a situation rather than describe something I personally experienced. References are included where they support specific technical claims or current events. Discussing a product or control does not mean I have personally deployed or tested it.

I am responsible for the final content, including its opinions, technical claims, and any mistakes. If you find something inaccurate or have a different experience, I welcome the feedback.
